## 2. Cross-cutting points

- **TED is above-threshold only.** For Czechia, Portugal, Romania (and the UK's Find a Tender) the ceilings that matter for splitting are national and below the EU thresholds, so TED rows cannot show splitting. The only check possible on TED data is "direct award without publication" counts: 20 (CZ), 63 (PT), 4 (RO), 23 (UK) rows shipped.
- **Shipped cohorts are small** (3 buyers per country, except France 672 buyers in `contracts.json` but only 28 flagged direct awards). Eligible counts below are for what ships today; full national files would be 100 to 1000 times larger.
- **Calibration** (HANDOFF item 10(d)): relationship hit rates measured on cartel cases are inflated, because those cases are discovered through bidder links, so the check "finds" what selected the sample. Calibrate instead on (1) convictions or audit findings that rest on independent evidence (leniency, whistleblower, wiretap, audit of bills of quantities), (2) matched controls from the same buyer, year and CPV, (3) a pre-registered threshold fixed before looking at labels, with lift and an interval as in the Ukraine work. For splitting there is no label set yet; candidate label sources: audit-body findings (Korea Board of Audit and Inspection, Chile Contraloría reports, Colombia Procuraduría decisions, Portuguese Tribunal de Contas audits, Romanian Curtea de Conturi / ANAP), Ukraine AMCU/State Audit Service findings. These source suggestions are from general knowledge and are **unverified**.
- **Personal data.** Beneficial-owner registers: the CJEU judgment of 22 Nov 2022, joined cases C-37/20 and C-601/20 (WM and Sovim), invalidated unrestricted public access ([curia.europa.eu press release](https://curia.europa.eu/jcms/jcms/p1_3610276/en)). Adverse history of natural persons falls under GDPR art. 10 (project rule, HANDOFF 10(e)). Names, birth dates, phones, e-mails of directors and bidders' contact persons should be used for matching in a private cache and never published; publish only company-level relations (shared owner entity, same address) with the reasoning, as `docs/adjudicated-outcomes.md` already designs.

## 3. South Korea (KONEPS)

**Thresholds (verified, official API of the national law database, consolidated text in force 2026-06-03).**
- National: 국가계약법 시행령 제26조제1항제5호: private contract allowed for construction up to KRW 400 m (general construction), KRW 200 m (specialist), KRW 160 m (other statutes); goods/services up to KRW 20 m; KRW 20 m to 100 m with small enterprises/micro-businesses and several other classes; lease up to KRW 50 m; KRW 50 m for young start-ups.
- Local: 지방계약법 시행령 제25조제1항제5호: same figures (나목: goods/services up to KRW 20 m; 라목: KRW 20 m to 100 m with small enterprises; 사목: lease up to KRW 50 m; 다목: KRW 20 to 50 m start-ups).
- Source: law.go.kr open API, `https://www.law.go.kr/DRF/lawService.do?OC=test&target=law&type=XML&LM=국가를당사자로하는계약에관한법률시행령` (and `...지방자치단체를당사자로하는계약에관한법률시행령`). Human pages: https://www.law.go.kr/법령/국가를당사자로하는계약에관한법률시행령 and https://www.law.go.kr/법령/지방자치단체를당사자로하는계약에관한법률시행령.
- Our open-standard data cite the ground in older letter form (`국가계약법 제26조제1항5호가2목` 141,558; `지방계약법 제25조제1항5호나목` 1,023,516), so a letter map is needed after the 2026 renumbering.

**Aggregation rule (verified).** Not a ban but an estimation rule: 시행령 제7조제3호 (national) and 제7조제2호 (local): where separate procurement demands are made "複數로 or 분할되어", the estimated price is either the total of similar contracts in the previous fiscal year / previous 12 months, adjusted for expected change, or the total to be contracted in the same fiscal year or the following 12 months. A works-specific ban exists: 국가계약법 시행령 제68조 (공사의 분할계약금지) and 지방계약법 시행령 제77조 (incl. 제77조제3항: no splitting in time or quantity to conclude 수의계약). 제29조/제28조 "분할수의계약" is the unrelated legal splitting of one contract among several suppliers. The one-quote vs two-quote procedure article (제30조) I did not retrieve: **unverified**.

**Data.** `opnstd.sqlite` (table `versions`/`contracts`): `buyer_code`, `buyer_name`, `signed`, `name` (item), `business_type`, `method`, `amount`, `legal_ref`, `supplier_bizrno`, `notice_no`, `joint`. 2,526,389 original contracts signed 2024-09-01..2026-09-01, of which 2,237,285 are 수의계약 across 22,833 buyers. Eligible-for-prototype (my count): private originals up to KRW 20 m, grouped by buyer and year, at least three contracts: 37,682 buyer-years, 1,567,463 contracts, 15,247 buyers; 35,164 of those groups sum above KRW 20 m (1,556,334 contracts, 14,689 buyers). There is no UNSPSC/item code in the open-standard file; text similarity on `name` plus `business_type` is the only same-object proxy. Budget line: not present.

**Related parties.** Needed: bid-opening results (`getOpengResultListInfo{Thng,Servc,Cnstwk,Frgcpt}`, data.go.kr 15129397, 1,000 calls/day, licence 제한 없음) with every bidder (per `research/korea-feasibility.md`; the exact bidder field names I did not confirm). Ownership: DART OpenAPI (free key, anyone can apply; https://opendart.fss.or.kr/intro/main.do) for listed and audited firms, includes the equity-disclosure group (DS004); daily limit and redistribution terms not retrieved (https://opendart.fss.or.kr/intro/terms.do, **unverified**). FTC corporate-group portal data on data.go.kr: 소속회사 overview, financials, shareholders (IDs 15091894, 15091895, 15091900; annual, as of 1 May; free application; https://www.data.go.kr/data/15091895/openapi.do). Small private firms (most 수의계약 suppliers; 81,488 individual-business suppliers) have no free ownership source. Same-address/phone checks were dropped at load for privacy (CEO name, phone, street address); a coarse check on city level only.
**Verdict.** A: implementable now. B: after named download (bid-opening results for competitive contracts; 70 to 88 percent of competitive contracts carry a notice number, per `research/korea-opnstd-profile.md`).
**False positives (A).** Local schools and offices legitimately buy the same recurring item monthly (meals, printing, cleaning) under a standing small-value rule; the one-year estimation rule means the right comparison is annual need per buyer department, which the data does not give. Hence a lead, not a score.

## 4. Ukraine (Prozorro)

**Thresholds.** Open-tender threshold for goods and services UAH 200,000, works UAH 1.5 m (Law 922-VIII on public procurement, art. 3 part 3; repo uses 200,000, `research/calibration/ukraine-candidate-indicators.md`; confirmed by a Ministry of Economy consultation found via search, https://me.gov.ua/InfoRez/Details/7f20f2db-17b2-4da1-bbeb-07b3b167369f?lang=uk-UA). Contracts from UAH 50,000 must be reported ("reporting" in our data; a Ministry note mentions this threshold changing with VAT treatment from 2026-07-01, https://me.gov.ua/inforez/Details/0ba19a1f-3419-4548-8e21-cd17efed936d?lang=uk-UA). **Partly verified:** `zakon.rada.gov.ua` was unreachable from this host (connection closed), so I could not read the article text itself. Anti-splitting: art. 3 part 10 para 2 (buyer must not divide the subject of procurement to avoid open tender, simplified procedures or separate provisions), cited in a Ministry consultation (https://me.gov.ua/InfoRez/Details/f49bb50a-fd9a-42ab-88c1-c21fa36d89c1?lang=uk-UA); exact wording **unverified**.
**Aggregation.** The law's notion is "subject of procurement" (same CPV class, per the Ministry guidance): unverified detail.
**Data (`data/prozorro.json`, 488 rows, 3 buyers).** `buyerId`, `cpv` (8-digit), `procedure` (`reporting` 424), `amount` (UAH), `date`, `supplierIds`, `lotId`, `description`. Prototype grouping (buyer, year, CPV, at least three reporting contracts under 200,000, sum at least 200,000): 4-digit CPV 0 groups, 3-digit CPV 1 group (5 rows, 1 buyer). The shipped sample is therefore unusable for tests; the cache (542 records under `data/prozorro/raw/records`, plus the free public API) would be needed. Ukrainian annual plans (Prozorro "plans") give the buyer's budget line; not in our files.
**Related parties.** Verified live on the public API (tender UA-2024-11-06-016205-a, 5 bids): `https://public-api.prozorro.gov.ua/api/2.5/tenders/{id}` returns every bid with `tenderers` carrying `identifier` (scheme `UA-EDR`, EDRPOU), `address`, `contactPoint` (name, phone, e-mail), `scale`. Our file stores only the count and `supplierIds` of the winner (181 of 488 are masked RNOKPP of individual entrepreneurs, 307 EDRPOU). Ownership: Unified State Register (ЄДР) open dataset on data.gov.ua, publisher Ministry of Justice, licence Creative Commons Attribution, updated weekly, XML ZIPs `UO.zip` (328 MB), `FOP.zip` (473 MB); the description lists founders, ultimate beneficial owner (or reason for absence), governing bodies, head and persons acting for the entity ([package](https://data.gov.ua/api/3/action/package_show?id=a1799820-195b-4982-8141-6e84f58103e7), page https://data.gov.ua/dataset/a1799820-195b-4982-8141-6e84f58103e7, last change 2026-10-13). A law-firm note says publication was suspended under martial law and restored on 19 Jan 2026 (https://www.crowe.com/ua/croweacu/news/registry-access-restored, secondary; page returned 403 to me). YouControl/Opendatabot are paid and not needed.
**Verdict.** A: implementable now, but only with a bigger Prozorro pull; weak prior (owner deferred, HANDOFF 7). B: implementable after named download (Prozorro bids for the calibration tenders + ЄДР UO.zip). Calibration on AMCU convictions is possible and already linked in the repo; use only decisions found without the link test, and remember AMCU decisions often come from bidder-link evidence (inflation).
**False positives.** B: shared phone or e-mail of a bookkeeping or tender-service firm; common registered-address service providers; founder names shared across unrelated shell-free businesses (name homonyms, no tax id for founders). A: recurring small purchases by large buyers (fuel, food); CPV class too coarse.

## 5. France (DECP)

**Thresholds (verified at the decree).** Code de la commande publique art. R2122-8: contracts without publicity or competition if EUR 60,000 excl. tax for supplies and services from 1 April 2026 (EUR 40,000 before), and EUR 100,000 for works from 1 Jan 2026 (décret n° 2025-1386 of 29 Dec 2025, art. 1 and 3): https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000053202067 (read: article 1 1° a/b, entry into force). The repo already encodes this in `script.js` lines 413 to 427 and `docs/score-v3.md` table. **Stale text:** `docs/score-v3.md` line 202 (splitting prototype) still reasons with EUR 40,000.
**Aggregation rule.** CCP art. R2121-5 (works: all works of the same operation), R2121-6 (supplies/services: homogeneous by their own characteristics or by a functional unit, regardless of number of operators or contracts) and the Conseil d'Etat rule that a buyer cannot escape a regime by splitting (decision of 4 Mar 2021, Edenred France, summarised at https://www.revuegeneraledudroit.eu/blog/decisions/conseil-detat-4-mars-2021-societe-edenred-france-requete-numero-438859). R2121-5/6 texts read via secondary sources (https://www.marche-public.fr/ccp/R2121-06-valeur-estimee-besoin-fourniture-services-homogenes-caracteristiques-propres-unite-fonctionnelle.htm); primary text **unverified**.
**Data.** `decp-history.json` (2,997 rows, 2 buyers), `decp-cities.json` (2,289 rows, 6 buyers), `contracts.json` (3,010 rows, 672 buyers, only 28 direct awards). Fields: `buyerSiret`/`buyer`, `cpv` (8-digit), `directAward`, `procedure`, `amount`, `date`, `description`, `lotId`, `frameworkId`, `supplierIds` (SIRET). Re-run of the old prototype with EUR 40,000 (my script): at least three direct awards under the threshold with sum at or above it, per buyer, year and CPV: 3-digit CPV 15 groups/89 rows (decp-history, 1 buyer), 6 groups/39 rows (decp-cities, 2 buyers); 5-digit CPV 8 groups/33 rows and 4 groups/20 rows. No operation or budget-line field. `description` is free text and could drive a same-object similarity step.
**Related parties.** Free API "Recherche d'entreprises" (the project already calls it): `dirigeants` with name, first names, birth year-month, role, and legal-person directors with SIREN, verified live for SIREN 329690648. RNE (INPI) is the source (`date_mise_a_jour_rne`). Licence of the API/RNE open data: **unverified**; a deputy's written question says INPI truncates day of birth and Decree 2025-840 of 22 Aug 2025 restricted some identity and address publication (https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/questions/QANR5L17QE12588.pdf, secondary). Beneficial owners (RBE): public access was kept in 2023 and then restricted to legitimate interest in the 2024 legislative amendments; current French status **unverified** (https://www.actu-juridique.fr/breves/entreprise/maintien-de-lacces-au-grand-public-du-registre-des-beneficiaires-effectifs/). DECP lists winners (titulaires) and an offers count, not bidders.
**Verdict.** A: implementable now as a lead only; adding 5-digit CPV plus description similarity plus same-supplier overlap is the realistic best, and it still cannot see the operation. B: after named download (dirigeants for the SIRENs of winners), only supplier-to-supplier and supplier-to-buyer-official links; no bidder links.
**False positives.** Large communes buy many small things under one CPV division; family of a mayor cannot be seen; common accountants/domiciliation firms as directors (legal-person directors).

## 6. Portugal (BASE + TED)

**Thresholds (verified, secondary law-firm summary of the new law).** Decreto-Lei n.º 177/2026, published 4 Sept 2026, in force **1 Oct 2026** for procedures started after that date (PLMJ note https://www.plmj.com/xms/files/03_Novidades_legislativas/2026/NI_CCP_2026.pdf, text extracted): direct award (ajuste direto) for works below EUR 150,000 (was 30,000); for movable goods and services below EUR 75,000 (was 20,000); other contracts below EUR 75,000 (was 50,000); concessions under one year below EUR 1,000,000 (was 75,000). Consulta prévia: works below EUR 1,000,000 (was 150,000), goods/services below EUR 130,000 (was 75,000). Simplified direct award (ajuste direto simplificado) rules in art. 128: EUR 5,000 recalled, **unverified**. Primary text not fetched (the Diário da República pages are script-rendered): https://diariodarepublica.pt.
**Aggregation / splitting rules.** CCP art. 22 on splitting (cited by a Portugal 2020 guidance note, https://poseur.portugal2020.pt/media/40611/alertas-%C3%A0s-entidades-benefici%C3%A1rias-no-%C3%A2mbito-da-contrata%C3%A7%C3%A3o-p%C3%BAblica.pdf, secondary). **A testable cumulative rule:** CCP art. 113(2): a buyer must not invite an entity that already received, in the current and the two preceding economic years, direct awards/consulta prévia whose accumulated contract price reaches the art. 19/20 limits (IMPIC guidance https://www.base.gov.pt/Base4/media/1pklcuvi/orientacao_tecnica_impic_ccp_final_2018-1.pdf, secondary; amounts and any 2026 change **unverified**). That is a supplier-level accumulation that the BASE fields can check directly.
**Data.** Shipped: `ted-portugal.json` (488 rows, 3 buyers; 63 negotiated-without-publication). Private: BASE contracts 2024 to 2026 (`data/portugal-base-*.json`, `docs/portugal-base.md`): `adjudicante`, `adjudicatarios`, `concorrentes` (lists "nine-digit NIF - name"), `cpv`, `tipoContrato`, procedure and criteria fields, three date fields, 3,966 rows retained in the buyer extraction; licence category "Outra (Domínio Público)" on dados.gov.pt (not a named standard licence; publication withheld by `docs/portugal-base-review.md`).
**Related parties.** BASE gives bidder NIFs per contract where filled (sometimes null) = co-bidding networks and a cheap "bidder pair always bids together" statistic. Ownership/shareholders: no free bulk source confirmed (the commercial register's public notices and the beneficial-owner register RCBE; access status **unverified**).
**Verdict.** A: implementable now from the private BASE cache (buyer NIF, CPV list, price, procedure, dates, supplier NIF); TED alone: not feasible. B: partly now (co-bidding), ownership: not feasible with free data. Do not publish BASE-derived output without the review the repo requires.
**False positives.** Many small municipalities and municipal companies under the same CPV; standing needs (maintenance, school meals); the new 2026 thresholds make direct award legal up to EUR 75,000/150,000, so splitting incentives mostly move to those values.

## 7. Romania (SEAP + TED)

**Thresholds (verified, consolidated text on legislatie.just.ro).** Law 98/2016 art. 7(5): the contracting authority may purchase directly (achiziție directă) products or services with estimated value excl. VAT **below RON 270,120** and works **below RON 900,400**; amended by Law 208/2022 (in force 10 Sept 2022): https://legislatie.just.ro/public/detaliidocument/178667 (consolidated history lists versions up to 17.11.2024; later changes 2025 to 2026 **unverified**; the earlier figures were 135,060 and 450,200).
**Aggregation / anti-splitting (verified).** Art. 11: (1) no calculation method to avoid award procedures; (2) the authority may not divide the contract into several distinct lower-value contracts, nor use calculation methods that undervalue, to avoid the procedures of the law. Estimated value calculation: paragraph 3 of the same section, articles 9 to 13 (cumulated estimated value of similar products/services; also HG 395/2016). Lot rule art. 19 (lots may use simplified procedure/direct purchase below RON 399,120 / 4,989,000 and up to 20 percent of total, per the 2018 text shown; current figures unverified).
**Data.** Shipped `ted-romania.json`: 321 rows, 3 buyers, 4 negotiated without publication, 316 open procedures, CUI, CPV. National SEAP (private cache `~/.cache/contract-signals/romania-seap`, about 208k contracts per HANDOFF; open data also on data.gov.ro "Achiziții publice 2023/2024/2025/2026", licence CC BY 4.0, https://data.gov.ro/dataset/achizitii-publice-2026 per CKAN API) holds direct purchases with authority CUI, CPV, value, date, winner only. HANDOFF flags that SEAP totals repeat framework ceilings per contract number; dedup before grouping.
**Related parties.** ONRC monthly open data on data.gov.ro, licence CC BY 4.0: `OD_FIRME.CSV` (name, CUI, registration no., address), `OD_REPREZENTANTI_LEGALI.CSV` (company id, person, role, birth date and place when given), `OD_STARE_FIRMA`, `OD_CAEN_AUTORIZAT` (https://data.gov.ro/dataset/firme-02-09-2026). Verified by header read. Associates/shareholders are not in the listed files (my inference; **unverified**). The beneficial-owner register access status after the CJEU ruling: **unverified**. SEAP gives winners only (Romanian TED data have `offers` count), so bidder links are not available except in rare decision documents; HANDOFF reports exactly this limit.
**Verdict.** A: implementable after a named step (use of the cached SEAP direct purchases with deduplicated framework ceilings); TED alone not feasible. B: after download of ONRC files, supplier-to-supplier links by shared legal representative or address among winners only.
**False positives.** Frameworks and subsequent contracts reported repeatedly; one common administrator for hundreds of companies (insolvency practitioners appear as "lichidator" in the file); pre-2022 thresholds were half the current ones.

## 8. Czechia (TED + national)

**Thresholds (verified, consolidated text of Act 134/2016 on zakonyprolidi.cz, read 2026-10-17).** § 27: small-scale public contract (veřejná zakázka malého rozsahu) up to CZK 3,000,000 for supplies/services and CZK 9,000,000 for works (excl. VAT, estimated value); below this the Act's award procedures do not apply. § 26: below-limit contracts are those above the § 27 values and under the EU limit of § 25. https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2016-134 (consolidated text; official publication on e-Sbirka not opened).
**Aggregation (verified).** § 18(1): a contract divided into parts is valued as the sum of all parts; (2): the sum must include all performance forming one functional whole and awarded in temporal connection; each part follows the procedure for the total value, except (3) parts under a government-regulation amount may follow their own value if together at most 20 percent of the total. § 19 (regular purchases: previous 12 months or next 12 months).
**Data.** `ted-czechia.json`: 630 rows, 3 buyers; ICO; 20 negotiated without publication; `lotId`, `cpv`, `amount`. National sub-threshold contracts: not shipped; a possible free national source is the contracts register (Registr smluv), **unverified**.
**Related parties.** ARES REST API (free, verified live): `https://ares.gov.cz/ekonomicke-subjekty-v-be/rest/ekonomicke-subjekty-vr/{ICO}` returns the commercial-register extract including `statutarniOrgany` (members: natural persons with address/functions) and `spolecnici` (partners with share amounts). Ministry of Justice open data of the registers at https://dataor.justice.cz (reachable; fields and licence **unverified**). Beneficial-owner register (evidence skutečných majitelů): closed to the public on 17 Dec 2025 and moving to a legitimate-interest regime (secondary: https://advokatnidenik.cz/2025/12/17/ministerstvo-spravedlnosti-znepristupni-evidenci-skutecnych-majitelu-firem/; https://advokatnidenik.cz/2026/06/23/pristup-k-udajum-z-evidence-skutecnych-majitelu-firem-budou-mit-jen-overeni-lide/). TED lists winners, not all bidders.
**Verdict.** A: not feasible on TED; unverified national source. B: after download of ARES records for winner ICOs: shared statutory-body member or partner among suppliers of the same buyer (partners and directors are public even without the closed owner register).
**False positives.** Professional nominee directors; municipal-company groups; the owner is a public body (state-owned groups) which is not a conflict.

## 9. United Kingdom (Find a Tender)

**Thresholds (secondary source for figures; Act verified).** Procurement Act 2023 thresholds from 1 Jan 2026 (incl. VAT): central government supplies/services GBP 135,018; sub-central GBP 207,720; works GBP 5,193,000 (law-firm summaries, e.g. https://cm.twobirds.com/en/insights/2025/uk/new-uk-public-procurement-thresholds-from-1st-january-2026; the statutory instrument itself not opened, **unverified**). Below the threshold the Act's competitive procedures are not required (no fixed direct-award ceiling as in the continental systems); direct award justifications in Schedule 5 apply above it (s. 41, https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/54/section/41).
**Aggregation (verified).** Schedule 3 para 4 ("Anti-avoidance"): where two or more contracts' goods, services or works could reasonably be supplied under a single contract, each contract's estimated value includes all of them, unless there are good reasons ([text](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/54/schedule/3/data.xml)). s. 4(3): a buyer must not use discretion in estimating value to avoid the Act. s. 5: mixed above/below-threshold contracts.
**Data.** `uk-fts.json`: 1,081 rows, 3 buyers; `buyerId`, `cpv`, `procedureDirect` (23 true), `amount`, `lotCount`; suppliers keyed by `GB-FTS` party id only (the note says a Companies House number is added "when published", yet all 1,081 rows carry only the FTS id). Below-threshold awards are on Contracts Finder, not in our files (licence and bulk access **unverified**).
**Related parties.** Companies House REST API (free; developer overview https://developer.company-information.service.gov.uk/overview, rate limits not retrieved) provides officers and PSC. **Verdict.** A: after named download (Contracts Finder). B: after named download (Companies House numbers from FTS OCDS + API); no bidder information.

## 10. Colombia (SECOP II)

**Thresholds.** Law 1150 of 2007 art. 2 (verified, text on the Senate site http://www.secretariasenado.gov.co/senado/basedoc/ley_1150_2007.html): "menor cuantía" depends on the entity's annual budget in SMMLV: 1,000 SMMLV (budget at least 1,200,000), 850, 650, 450 (budget 120,000 to under 400,000), 280 (budget below 120,000). Mínima cuantía = up to 10 percent of the entity's menor cuantía (art. 2 num. 5, added by Law 1474/2011, changed by Law 2069/2020 art. 30; confirmed via the Colombia Compra concept https://relatoria.colombiacompra.gov.co/conceptos/c-246-de-2025/; the numeral text itself is not in the consolidated page I read, **unverified**). With SMMLV 2026 = COP 1,750,905 (Decree 1469 of 2025; a Council of State suspension and Decree 159 of 19 Feb 2026 kept the value; secondary sources, e.g. https://consultorsalud.com/aumento-del-salario-minimo-en-colombia-2026/): mínima cuantía ceilings range from 28 SMMLV (COP 49.0 m) to 100 SMMLV (COP 175.1 m). Direct hiring (contratación directa) has no value ceiling; it depends on a ground (e.g. professional services, no plurality). Anti-splitting provision: **unverified** (I did not find the article).
