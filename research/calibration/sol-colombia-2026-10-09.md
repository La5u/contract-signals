**Recommendation: build a small label-first cohort from criminal court judgments, retaining only confirmed final convictions and uniquely resolved SECOP II contracts.** None of the examined sources provides a verified, ready-made corruption-label table keyed to SECOP II.

Judgments offer the strongest alignment with the intended outcome. Start with **20–50 candidates**—a proposed pilot, not an estimated yield—and resolve each numbered contract to one SECOP II ID. Record offense, judgment URL, finality evidence and linkage confidence. Monitor Ciudadano can supply leads.

Compare indicator rates against matched **ordinary, unlabelled** contracts; do not call them clean. Keep contractual sanctions and fiscal liability as separate targets, consistent with the [existing label policy](../../research/labels/README.md).
