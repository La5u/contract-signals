**1. Access, exports, size and terms.** Annual ZIP exports exist. The official 2025 listing reports **358.94 MB**, updated daily. The documented archive contains UTF-8, tab-separated CSV metadata (`documents.csv`) and classification dictionaries; `doc_url` points to separately hosted **HTML judgment text**. It is therefore not a self-contained full-text corpus. Total 2018–2026 storage, including texts, is **unverified**. Sources: [DSA listing](https://dsa.court.gov.ua/dsa/inshe/oddata/879/), [export specification](https://data.gov.ua/dataset/dedce089-cf4b-4adf-97dc-1c5152080d05/resource/2f7be0b6-53c8-4635-950d-f5cdc4b7a25b/revision/287339/download).

Data.gov.ua offers a **JSON catalogue API** (`package_show`, `resource_show`); I found no documented official judgment-search API. No numerical request limits were verified. The [registry](https://reyestr.court.gov.ua/) explicitly uses interactive protection against automated searching/downloading and currently advertises restricted access. The [dataset](https://data.gov.ua/dataset/ediniy-derzhavniy-reestr-sudovih-rishen-za-2025-rik_879) declares Creative Commons Attribution and permits commercial reuse with source hyperlinks; its licence version is unspecified. [API instructions](https://data.gov.ua/faq/13).

Category taxonomies also distinguish pre-/post-2019 cases. [Registry search](https://reyestr.court.gov.ua/).

The documented export has **no finality field**: `status=1` means published/active, not final. Courts transmit dates of entry into legal force, but completeness and a public finality filter remain unverified. **4. Personal data and reuse.** Public judgments redact identifying personal information, with statutory exceptions; observed texts use `ОСОБА_`, `АДРЕСА_`, `ІНФОРМАЦІЯ_`. Article 6 permits reproduction/databases without distorting meaning; Article 7 governs redaction. [Law](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/3262-15). The export specification additionally requires applications to update removed decisions **daily**. Recommended practice: retain contract IDs, outcome passages and provenance; preserve redactions.
