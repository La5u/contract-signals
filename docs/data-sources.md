## Portugal BASE research source — not an explorer dataset

IMPIC's [official national contract export](https://dados.gov.pt/datasets/contratos-publicos-portal-base-impic-contratos-de-2012-a-2026) labels its category `other-pd`; the official licence catalogue identifies this as **“Outra (Domínio Público)” (Other/Public Domain)**, without a terms URL. It is not CC0, and the declaration does not clear personal-data or incorporated-content rights. BASE's responsible-use page recommends dados.gov.pt for raw downloads.

Annual 2024–2026 ZIPs are held privately outside Git/the website. Only bounded prefixes have been inspected; no BASE records are published or scored. Official excerpts/hashes: [`../data/portugal-base-rights.json`](../data/portugal-base-rights.json). Field meanings, minimization policy and remaining publisher questions: [portugal-base.md](portugal-base.md). Do not transfer the TED free-reuse label or TED scoring rules to these national-source records.
