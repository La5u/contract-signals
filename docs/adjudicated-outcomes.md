# Independent outcomes, not corruption predictions

**Current labelled positives: zero** in the provided French, Colombian and Paraguayan cohorts. No source in these extracts establishes a final contract-specific corruption judgment. The eight French CRC dossiers are *official audit observations*, including five concerning sets of orders; they are not criminal convictions and must not be relabelled. Absence of an outcome field means **not established/reviewed in this extract**, never “not corrupt”. An empty judgment filter does not estimate prevalence or model accuracy.

A future row may include a `corruptionOutcome` only after manual documentary verification that:

1. A competent adjudicative authority has issued a **final** decision expressly establishing a corruption offence/conduct (not an allegation, investigation, general sanction, audit criticism or ordinary procedural irregularity). The exact offence and adjudicative authority are recorded. Recheck finality, appeal and reversal status; remove or update the label if later evidence changes it.
2. The judgment expressly identifies the **particular contract** represented by the row, or an independently documented, unambiguous identifier chain establishes the link. Same buyer, supplier, project or similar amount is not enough. An aggregate finding cannot be propagated to each procurement in the aggregate. A contract-linked judgment does not imply guilt of every supplier/person named on a row.
3. Record `status: "final-adjudication"`, `scope: "contract"`, `contractId` matching the row, `authority`, `decisionId`, `decisionDate`, `judgmentUrl` (official decision), `finalityUrl` (evidence of finality), `verifiedAsOf`, `sourcePassage` (decision passage), `offence` and `linkageBasis`. Both links must be HTTP(S). `validateContracts` rejects incomplete or aggregate labels. The documents themselves and later changes still require human review.

A separate `investigationReports` list records **dated, contract-specific press reports** about investigative steps. Each report must give a dated source URL, event date, publisher, exact dossier linkage and `status: "reported-at-date-current-unknown"`. This is not an adjudication, proof of guilt, or a claim that an inquiry is still open. Currently only `crc-station-nuage` is tagged: [Presse Océan / maville (11 September 2024)](https://labaule.maville.com/actu/actudet_-une-perquisition-a-la-mairie-de-saint-sebastien-sur-loire-pour-un-contrat-lie-a-la-station-nuage-_dep-6456332_actu.Htm) reported that the municipality confirmed a 10 September search related to Station Nuage. Its reported investigative step is distinct from the CRC audit finding on that dossier. A later outcome or closure has **not** been verified. Press about Fernando de la Mora's municipal finances does not identify a contract in the Paraguay pilot, so none was tagged; no Colombian row has an independently linked report in this review. Do not propagate reporting to other purchases by the same buyer or supplier.

The outcome label is **never a scoring input**, and the UI filters/badges for adjudications, reported investigations and “official audit findings” are distinct. Jurisdiction-specific adjudication rules and public-access/republication rights must be checked before adding a real labelled case. To compare triage performance across countries, first build independently reviewed positive and *negative* samples with comparable selection and reporting coverage; treating every unlabeled row as negative would bias the result.
