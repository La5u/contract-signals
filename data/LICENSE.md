# Data reuse and attribution

The root [MIT licence](../LICENSE) applies to the project's code and original documentation. It does **not** relicense third-party procurement records, raw snapshots, register extracts or source documents.

Files in this directory retain their publishers' licences and reuse conditions. The source-by-source register is [docs/data-sources.md](../docs/data-sources.md); retained official licence evidence is [docs/source-rights-evidence.json](../docs/source-rights-evidence.json). Coverage files identify publishers, collection dates and cohort scope.

When redistributing records or derived datasets:

- Attribute the original publisher and identify the source and retrieval date.
- Preserve applicable licence notices and identify modifications.
- Observe any applicable share-alike conditions, particularly for Colombian CC BY-SA material.
- Do not imply publisher endorsement or treat a data licence as privacy clearance.

Third-party attachments and reproduced source documents can have separate rights. Unresolved scope and personal-data publication questions are recorded in [docs/personal-data.md](../docs/personal-data.md).

This file is a notice of source-dependent terms, not a new licence for all data.
