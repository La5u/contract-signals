Dataset

    France · DECP contracts of eight buyers (Ville de Paris, Ardèche, Rennes, Nantes, Bordeaux, Grenoble, Dijon, Tours), notified 2024–2025: 5,286 contracts. Amounts are compared across the Ministry dataset, buyer feeds and open-data lists; disagreements show as ranges. Coverage depends on source publication. Supplier names are current register names.

    Datasets are not merged; they may describe the same purchases. Do not add their amounts. “—” means unknown, not safe. Coverage and provenance (JSON).

      Signals highlight published characteristics for review, not established irregularities.

      The index orders records using the available indicators. “Not assessed” means no applicable check could be evaluated; 0 means no threshold crossed in the evaluated checks. Missing values are shown as unknown.
